# Contrast Coffee

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are students and remote workers deciding which Contrast Coffee location best supports a study or work session. The website is maintained by a small agency team for Contrast Coffee.

## Product Purpose

This is Contrast Coffee's production-ready official public website. It must faithfully translate the existing brand into a distinctive, high-performance digital experience, clearly communicate the study/work café concept, menu, spaces, and locations, and help customers choose a location and open map directions.

Success means supporting real customer visits from launch while providing a strong foundation for future brand growth.

## Positioning

Contrast Coffee is not positioned as a conventional café. It is a study-and-work environment built around focus, persistence, discipline, and the ability to work at any hour. “Kiên Trì - Kỷ Luật” is a core brand proposition. The 24/7 concept is part of the brand position, while exact operating hours remain location-specific facts that require current first-party verification.

## Operating Context

Visitors use the website to:

- understand Contrast Coffee's study and work café concept;
- browse current menu information;
- evaluate spaces for studying or remote work;
- compare current locations and verified opening hours;
- choose a location and open map directions.

The launch language is Vietnamese.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single-customer repository dedicated to Contrast Coffee.
- Public production website, not an agency concept demo.
- Launch scope covers menu, spaces, locations, and map/directions links.
- No backend, ordering, reservation, authentication, or database is required for launch unless later confirmed. Public page and route structure remains an implementation decision and may evolve where useful for usability, shareability, or SEO.
- Operational facts such as menu items, prices, locations, and opening hours require current first-party verification.
- Unverified operational facts must be marked provisional or omitted, never invented.
- Existing Vite, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, shadcn/Base UI, Motion, GSAP, Lenis, and Playwright stack remains the implementation foundation.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve Contrast Coffee's first-party identity unless Contrast Coffee explicitly approves a change:

- logo and wordmark;
- established brand colors;
- tone of voice;
- photography and image treatment;
- approved campaign language.

Vietnamese brand voice is concise, direct, disciplined, energetic, and occasionally playful. It may use study, work, progress, discipline, deadline, and youth-culture language, but should not become corporate, inspirational-generic, luxury, or cute. Existing approved campaign language is the authority for future copy.

Preserving the brand does not require reproducing existing campaign layouts literally. Digital interaction, motion, spatial composition, navigation behavior, and responsive systems may extend the identity where they remain recognizably Contrast.

The website should feel like a faithful digital expression of Contrast Coffee, not a category-generic café design.

## Evidence on Hand

Primary factual authority:

- official Contrast Coffee Facebook;
- official Contrast Coffee Instagram;
- official Contrast Coffee TikTok and Linktree;
- current first-party menus;
- current location materials;
- approved campaign assets;
- information supplied or confirmed directly by Contrast Coffee.

Third-party articles, reviews, map listings, and case studies may support discovery and context. When facts conflict, the latest first-party source wins.

No verified menu, price, location, opening-hour, testimonial, award, certification, or performance dataset is currently stored in this repository. Future work must not fabricate these claims.

## Product Principles

1. Preserve recognizable Contrast Coffee brand equity.
2. Help visitors choose a suitable location quickly.
3. Make operational facts current, clear, and verifiable.
4. Treat mobile performance and accessibility as launch requirements.
5. Prefer a distinctive, focused public experience over unnecessary product architecture.
6. Digital expression should extend the existing brand rather than merely reproduce its print and social-media artifacts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The public website must use semantic HTML, keyboard-accessible interactions, visible focus states, WCAG AA contrast, usable touch targets, descriptive alternative text, and reduced-motion support.
