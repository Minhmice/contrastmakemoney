# Homepage Surface Brief

## Scope and Visitor Mode

- **Target:** Contrast Coffee public homepage.
- **Visitor mode:** Persuade with Experience-level craft.
- **Audience:** Vietnamese students and remote workers deciding where to study or work.
- **Job:** Understand what Contrast is, experience its focus-and-release proposition, choose a suitable location, and open directions.
- **Surface thesis:** A continuous Contrast performance rather than a stack of conventional café landing-page sections.

## Outcome and Action

The initial viewport must make Contrast intelligible as a study-and-work café environment shaped by focus, persistence, discipline, youth, and energy. The performative layer must increase desire without hiding the offer.

The primary action is to choose a verified location and open map directions. Menu, space, and time exploration support that decision. Action labels remain provisional until final Vietnamese copy is approved.

## Proof and Content

Use current first-party Contrast Coffee materials as authority:

- approved campaign language, including “Kiên Trì - Kỷ Luật”;
- master logo, red-square geometry, mascot, and canonical brand assets;
- current menu and prices;
- verified location names, addresses, hours, and map links;
- authentic photography of spaces, customers studying or working, products, lifestyle, and campaigns.

Third-party material may guide discovery but cannot override current first-party operational facts. Missing proof is a replacement requirement, not permission to invent content.

## Selected Direction and Continuous Performance

The homepage applies **Controlled Contrast** through one continuous experience. The chapters describe visitor understanding and transformation, not a mandatory row of separate card sections.

### Noise / Focus

The visitor enters a composition under controlled visual tension. Typography, image, focus, alignment, or cursor response may create temporary disruption before the system resolves. Within the first viewport, the visitor still understands what Contrast is and sees a clear route toward choosing a location.

### Kiên Trì - Kỷ Luật

The proposition is performed rather than merely displayed. Scroll, timing, masking, interruption, recovery, or interaction should turn persistence and discipline into an observable behavior. The sequence must remain concise enough that product clarity is not delayed.

### The Space Works With You

Real photography and spatial composition present Contrast as an active environment for focus, study, work, and social energy. Atmosphere and architecture lead. Supporting facts stay verifiable and legible.

### Fuel

Menu content becomes part of working rhythm without inventing pseudo-scientific states or unsupported product claims. Product color, photography, interaction, and campaign humor may create release from the stricter study narrative. Current first-party menu and prices remain authority.

### Time

The 24/7 brand concept becomes a temporal digital system through verified operating data, lighting, rhythm, live time, or time-of-day behavior. The experience must not imply that every location shares identical hours. Exact hours remain location-specific facts.

### Choose Your Contrast

Locations become a clear decision interface. Visitors compare verified address, hours, atmosphere, relevant space information, and map access without decoding an art piece. Interaction may be performative, but selection and orientation remain immediate.

### Go

The ending resolves the performance into action. The chosen location and directions become dominant, direct, and easy to activate.

This narrative belongs to the homepage only. Other routes inherit DESIGN.md without copying this sequence.

## Signature Behavior and Motion

The global homepage behavior is **ORDER → INTERRUPTION → RESPONSE → RESOLUTION**.

Motion may use four states:

1. **CONTROL:** Precise alignment, tracking, masking, timing, grid lock, and clean reveals.
2. **BREAK:** Typography escapes, photography bleeds, a red field interrupts, a cursor displaces, or structure temporarily misregisters.
3. **RECOVER:** Type, images, navigation, geometry, and information resolve into a legible controlled state.
4. **RELEASE:** Product, mascot, menu, photography, labels, or micro-interactions introduce lighter energy and relief.

A logo-derived red square may connect chapters as a marker, cursor state, mask, active indicator, temporal object, or transition anchor. It must change state or orientation rather than repeat as static decoration.

Appropriate tools include GSAP for one or two major pinned or scrubbed narrative transformations, Motion for local state and interaction, Lenis only when synchronized smooth scrolling materially improves choreography, and CSS for direct feedback. Tool ownership must remain explicit so libraries do not animate the same property on the same element.

Stillness is part of the rhythm. Do not animate every chapter, every heading, or every image. Motion exists to express control, break, recovery, release, hierarchy, feedback, or state.

## Performative Interaction Rules

- Cursor behavior may manipulate brand geometry, reveal content, disturb and recover a stable composition, or create meaningful contrast. Provide a complete non-pointer alternative.
- Parallax may support photographic atmosphere and depth when restrained, directional, and tied to composition.
- Blur may communicate distraction becoming focus or transient depth. It must not become decorative glass UI.
- Gradients may arise from photography, light, heat, time, depth, transition, or movement rather than generic decoration.
- One or two pinned sequences may carry major narrative transformations. The entire homepage must not become a pinned slideshow.
- Modular compositions may resemble bento structures only when cells differ meaningfully in hierarchy, scale, material, content, or behavior.
- Curved geometry may support drinkware, products, people, controls, physics, or deliberate tension against the square brand system.

## Responsive and Accessibility Constraints

- The first mobile and desktop viewport communicates the proposition and exposes a clear location/directions path.
- Mobile is a recomposition: shorter spatial sequences, alternate crops, horizontal type, touch-driven states, and reduced cursor dependence.
- No essential information, navigation, or action exists only inside animation, hover, cursor response, pinned timing, or live-time behavior.
- Location facts, menu content, and map actions use semantic, keyboard-accessible controls with visible focus and usable touch targets.
- Vietnamese diacritics remain intact in every display, mask, transform, crop, and animation state.
- Photography reserves dimensions to prevent layout shift and uses meaningful Vietnamese alternative text.
- Reduced motion preserves hierarchy, story, contrast, navigation, and state. Complex sequences become direct state changes, short transforms, fades, or static compositions.
- Smooth scrolling must not delay input, impede keyboard navigation, or make touch scrolling sluggish.

## Boundaries

- Preserve PRODUCT.md and DESIGN.md as authorities for product truth and global visual rules.
- Do not define final tokens, fonts, production components, or route architecture in this brief.
- Do not fabricate menu items, prices, locations, hours, testimonials, awards, community metrics, or performance claims.
- Do not use generic stock café photography where authentic Contrast imagery is available.
- Do not reduce the homepage to `Hero → About → Menu → Space → Locations` with interchangeable section templates.
- Do not make it a generic café site, SaaS landing page, animated poster archive, cursor demo, or technical showreel.
- Do not reproduce current posters literally when digital interaction can extend the identity more effectively.

## Unresolved Decisions

- Canonical Contrast red from master vector or logo source.
- Final Vietnamese display and body typefaces with complete diacritic support.
- Approved homepage copy, action labels, and chapter naming.
- Verified launch menu, locations, hours, map links, and photography inventory.
- Available mascot assets and approved interaction boundaries.
- Whether verified data supports live time or location-specific time-of-day behavior.
- Whether content volume and SEO justify dedicated menu or location routes.
- Exact ownership, duration, and performance budget for each major motion sequence after content and assets are known.
