# Menu Surface Brief

## Scope and Visitor Mode

- **Target:** Dedicated `/menu` utility route.
- **Visitor mode:** Operate with restrained Persuade craft.
- **Audience:** Vietnamese students and remote workers who want to check what is available, compare prices, and return to their visit decision quickly.
- **Job:** Scan categories and items without passing through a performative homepage sequence.
- **Primary action:** Read verified menu information; secondary action is choosing a location.

## Proof, Content, and Constraints

- The latest first-party Contrast menu is the only authority for item names, descriptions, sizes, availability, and prices.
- Current entries are explicitly labelled layout illustrations and must not be published as operational facts.
- Preserve semantic category navigation, keyboard access, visible focus, Vietnamese diacritics, mobile scanning, and reduced motion.
- Do not turn the route into a product-card grid or invent drink photography; authentic cutouts may be introduced only when supplied.

## Chosen Direction

The route is a **working menu ledger**: a quiet paper catalogue with strict horizontal rules, a sticky category index, and one ink/red opening gesture. It inherits Controlled Contrast but keeps performance subordinate to scanning. The memorable moment is a red menu sheet wiping and locking into the black half of the first viewport; after that, the interface becomes stable and utility-first.

## Unresolved Decisions

- Verified first-party categories, item names, descriptions, sizes, prices, and availability.
- Whether authentic product cutouts exist for selected hero items.
- Whether verified content volume warrants per-category URLs or structured menu metadata.
